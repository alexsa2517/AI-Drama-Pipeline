# MYTHIC ARCHIVES V1

## Channel position

**MYTHIC ARCHIVES** is a faceless, English-language cinematic mythology channel.

Tagline: **Forgotten Legends. Cinematic Stories.**

The channel does not compete as a generic "AI channel." AI is the production method; mythology is the product.

## Production rule

`Research → Story Score → Episode YAML → Character Bible → Scene Visual Lock → Image → Video → Voice → Audio → QA → Human Review`

The existing AI Drama Pipeline remains the production engine. V1 adds an editorial layer before episode generation.

## Editorial gate

A story should normally score **75/100 or higher** before production:

- Hook — 25%
- Curiosity — 20%
- Emotional impact — 15%
- Myth significance — 15%
- Visual potential — 15%
- Originality — 10%

The score is an editorial quality gate, not a prediction of views or subscribers.

## Truth labeling

Research notes use four labels:

- `FACT` — supported historical/material evidence.
- `TRADITION` — a documented belief, myth, or traditional account.
- `INTERPRETATION` — scholarly or editorial interpretation.
- `DRAMATIZATION` — invented detail added for cinematic storytelling.

The final script must not blur these categories.

## Shorts retention design

Target: 45–60 seconds.

1. 0–3s — hook
2. 3–10s — mystery
3. 10–25s — escalation
4. 25–40s — reveal
5. 40–52s — meaning/twist
6. 52–60s — truthful open loop

## Visual identity

- Cinematic historical-fantasy realism.
- Strong environmental continuity.
- Controlled camera movement.
- Distinctive lighting motivated by the mythic setting.
- Consistent character identity across scenes.
- No random visual spectacle that contradicts the story.

## Expansion rule

The channel starts with mythology and legends only. General news, broad knowledge, finance, AI tutorials, and unrelated entertainment are excluded from the core channel.

If a future topic does not strengthen the mythology promise, it belongs in another channel rather than being added here.
